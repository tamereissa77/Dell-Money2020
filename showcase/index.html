Why on-device matters in this market

Data residency stops being a blocker

Under SAMA supervision and the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), moving customer records to a foreign cloud region is a multi-quarter procurement hurdle. When the entire model, database, and inference stack runs where the data already lives, that conversation never starts.

Small enough to put in a branch

This is a quiet desktop machine, not a data centre rack. The same stack runs in a retail branch, a regional processing centre, or a primary vault — without redesigning HVAC, power delivery, or physical security around it.
